About

The Vision

Technicolour Thoughts is where ideas burst into vivid life. Every frame is a canvas, every edit a brushstroke, painting stories that resonate and move.

We believe in the power of visual storytelling to transform, inspire, and connect. From music videos that pulse with energy to documentaries that dig deep into the human experience.

The Craft

Based in Aotearoa New Zealand, we bring a unique perspective to every project. Blending technical excellence with creative intuition, we craft visuals that don't just capture attention—they hold it.

Whether it's a bold music video, a heartfelt documentary, or something entirely new, we approach each project with fresh eyes and an open mind.

The Person

Produced by Joel Tempero—a creative who sees the world a little differently. With a passion for colour, movement, and emotion, Joel transforms concepts into compelling visual narratives.
